FAQs

Questions we get
all the time.

Still not sure? These are the questions we answer most often — and the honest answers behind them.

Ask Us Directly
How much does a typical garden job cost?
Every garden is different, so we don't publish fixed prices — a small lawn maintenance visit is a world apart from a full garden design and paving project. What we will always do is give you a free, itemised written quote in euros before any work begins. No vague estimates, no "we'll see how it goes." You'll know exactly what you're paying for.
Do you show up on time? (Seriously.)
Yes — and we understand why you're asking. Unreliable tradespeople are one of Ireland's great frustrations. We're not perfect, but we communicate proactively: if something comes up, you'll hear from us before the start time, not after. Our customers tell us this alone makes us stand out.
How quickly can you start?
It depends on the job and time of year. For smaller work like lawn mowing or power washing, we can often be with you within a week. Larger projects (paving, decking, artificial grass installations) may take a couple of weeks to schedule. We'll give you an honest timeframe when you call — not an optimistic one we can't keep.
Are you fully insured?
Yes — fully. We carry public liability insurance and can provide documentation on request. Never hire a landscaping contractor who can't confirm their insurance status. It protects both of you.
What happens if I'm not happy with the finished result?
We do a walkthrough with you before we leave and sign off. If anything isn't to your satisfaction, we come back and fix it — no charge, no argument. In 20 years of business we've had very few callbacks, but when we do, we take them seriously. Our reputation is built on how we handle the 1% of jobs that don't go perfectly.
Do I need to be home for the work?
For regular maintenance like lawn mowing, no — many of our customers give us access to their back garden and we work while they're out. For larger projects we prefer someone to be contactable, but you don't necessarily need to be on-site all day. We'll discuss the best approach when we do your initial survey.
Do you work in winter?
Yes — Irish winters are mild enough that block paving, power washing, artificial grass installation and many other jobs continue year-round. Lawn mowing slows down but doesn't stop completely. Winter is also a great time to plan and quote for spring projects — so you're first in the queue when the season picks up.
